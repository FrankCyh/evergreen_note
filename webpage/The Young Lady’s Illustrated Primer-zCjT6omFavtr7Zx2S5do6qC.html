The Young Lady’s Illustrated Primer

(This turned into a full essay here. These notes are now pretty outdated.)

As described in The Diamond Age, the Young Lady’s Illustrated Primer (often just called “The Primer”) is a fictional electronic book which facilitates the remote, asynchronous education of children. Its curriculum includes practical skills like math and reading, principally via Inquiry-based learning, but The Primer’s goal is to produce creative, subversive youth.

For many in educational technology, the Primer represents an inspiring vision for a future of education:

  • The Primer reminds people of their own experiences with profound enablement
  • The Primer doesn’t appear to coerce its students
  • The Primer is scalable

Unfortunately, I don’t think it’s a viable vision: The Primer isn’t a viable enabling environment.


References
