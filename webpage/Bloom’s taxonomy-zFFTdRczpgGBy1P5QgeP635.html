Bloom’s taxonomy

Benjamin S. Bloom’s classic taxonomy of learning tasks. In its contemporary form:

  1. Remember
  2. Understand
  3. Apply
  4. Analyze
  5. Evaluate
  6. Create

The current updated treatment, with lots of vignettes applying and analyzing this taxonomy in the context of various real-world lessons plans:
Anderson, L. W., & Krathwohl, D. R. (Eds.). (2001). A taxonomy for learning, teaching, and assessing: A revision of Bloom’s taxonomy of educational objectives. Longman
