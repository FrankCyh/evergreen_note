The Primer++

The Primer++ isn’t one particular book, like Stephenson’s The Young Lady’s Illustrated Primer. It’s a new form of environments for thinking and communicating, instantiated in a variety of ways across fields, topics, and contexts. And… it’s probably not actually a primer (“Primer” is an unsituated framing).
